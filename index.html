YouAt

A private, family-first location-sharing app for iOS and Android.

What YouAt does

YouAt lets a small circle of family members share their real-time location with each other — and only with each other. No advertisers, no data resale, no tracking. The app is designed so nobody outside your invited group can see where you are.

The app is used by parents, spouses, and small households to coordinate day-to-day logistics: confirming a child arrived at school, checking a partner's ETA home, finding a family member in a crowded venue.

Key features

  • Groups — invite family via one-time code; only invited members see each other's location
  • Ghost Mode — pause sharing at any time from Settings
  • Zones — get gentle arrival/departure notifications for a home or school
  • Check-ins — request a "safe" confirmation from a family member
  • SOS — one-tap alert to your group in an emergency
  • Where did I park? — remembers where you left your car

How we use SMS

YouAt sends a single one-time verification code when a user signs into the app on a new device. That is the only type of SMS YouAt ever sends. See our SMS Consent page for the full opt-in flow, sample message, and opt-out instructions.
